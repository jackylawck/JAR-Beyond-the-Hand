# Export Control & Technology Classification Self-Declaration
# 出口管制與雙用途技術符合性自我宣告

---

## 1. Regulatory Alignment / 法規對標
This declaration confirms the technology classification of **J.A.R. Beyond the Hand 3D** against international export control regimes:
1. **Wassenaar Arrangement (WA)** on Export Controls for Conventional Arms and Dual-Use Goods and Technologies.
2. **U.S. Export Administration Regulations (EAR)** / Commerce Control List (CCL).
3. **European Union Dual-Use Regulation (EU) 2021/821**.
4. **Hong Kong Strategic Commodities Regulations** (Cap. 60 sub. leg. G).
5. **PRC Export Control Law (中華人民共和國出口管制法)** & Dual-Use Items Catalog.

---

## 2. Technical Classification & Exemption Basis / 技術分類與豁免依據

### EN:
- **Pure Public Domain & Fundamental Research**: All inverse kinematics routines (Cyclic Coordinate Descent with Damped Least Squares - CCD-DLS) implemented herein represent well-documented, standard mathematical algorithms widely published in open scientific literature.
- **ECCN Determination**: Not subject to military-grade ECCNs (e.g., Category 2B007 / 2B207 robotic systems with special environmental hardening). Classified as **EAR99** / Publicly Available Technology under 15 CFR § 734.7.
- **No Embedded Weapon Guidance**: The codebase contains zero proprietary missile guidance laws, real-time inertial tactical navigation payloads, or ballistic fire-control integration matrices.

### 中文:
- **公開領域與基礎科學研究豁免**：本代碼庫中實現的循環坐標下降法與阻尼最小二乘演算法（CCD-DLS），均屬於國際公認、公開發表的經典幾何運動學數理邏輯。
- **出口管制編號判定**：不屬於受控的軍事或特種環境加固機器人清單（如 2B007 / 2B207 等級）。歸類為通用開源公開技術（EAR99 等效），符合公眾可用性技術豁免標準。
- **無受控制導模組**：系統不包含任何武器制導代碼、戰術慣性導航運算或火控對接介面。
